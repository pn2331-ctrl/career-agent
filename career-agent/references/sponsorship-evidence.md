# Sponsorship evidence

Use this reference when work authorization or future sponsorship affects screening. This is evidence recording, not legal advice.

## Evidence labels

| Label | Evidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YES — direct evidence | Current JD or official employer policy explicitly supports sponsorship | Quote or link current support |
| NO — direct evidence | Current JD or official policy explicitly refuses sponsorship or requires a status the user cannot meet | Quote or link current refusal |
| UNCLEAR — positive historical signal | Current role is silent, but the same employer entity has verified H-1B history | A prioritization signal, not a promise |
| UNCLEAR — no verified signal | No direct current evidence or reliable historical signal | Do not infer a result |

Do not use company size, reputation, industry, recruiter title, or silence as evidence.

## Investigation order

1. Check the current JD and official employer careers or immigration-policy pages.
2. If current evidence is absent and sponsorship matters, check reliable historical data such as official USCIS employer petition data or DOL OFLC disclosure data.
3. Match the legal employer entity, not only the brand. Record source URL, access date, entity name, time period, and whether the record is a petition approval, filing, or labor-condition application.
4. Use the correct label and state what the record does not establish.

An LCA is not an approved H-1B petition. Historical approvals or filings do not prove that this opening, team, location, or future policy supports sponsorship.

## Output wording

- YES — direct evidence: exact current wording.
- NO — direct evidence: exact current wording.
- UNCLEAR — positive historical signal: current JD is silent; source shows H-1B activity for the matched entity in a stated period. This is not opening-specific.
- UNCLEAR — no verified signal: current JD is silent and no entity-matched reliable signal was found.
