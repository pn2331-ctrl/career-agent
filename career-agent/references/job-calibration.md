# Target-JD calibration

Use this reference during setup when the user supplies favorite, previously applied, representative, or rejected job descriptions.

Store each JD or its permitted notes under `job_calibration`. For each one, extract only:

- title, role family, location, seniority, and work-model signals;
- recurring responsibilities, evidence types, tools, and language;
- hard requirements versus preferences;
- the user's reason for liking or rejecting it;
- coverage by the current fact inventory and skills inventory: label each material JD term `directly supported`, `transferable`, or `unsupported`.

Compare several examples before naming a role family. A role family must be a coherent professional identity with shared evidence order, not a collection of adjacent keywords. Write its proposed identity, boundaries, evidence order, and gaps in `role_family_design` and ask the user to approve it.

Do not use a JD to create an unsupported candidate claim or skill. A JD reveals what an employer values; the fact and skills inventories determine what the candidate can truthfully say.
