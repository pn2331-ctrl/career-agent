# Skills inventory

Extract every user-stated skill, tool, method, domain, credential, and deliverable from all source resumes and confirmed materials before selecting role-family skills.

| Item | Type | Supporting source or fact record | State | Role-family relevance | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

States: `Confirmed`, `Source-faithful`, `Evidence-limited`, `Conflict-open`.

JD terms are mapped later as `directly supported`, `transferable`, or `unsupported`; a JD term never creates a new skill.
